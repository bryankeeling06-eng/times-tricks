# Times Tricks – saved idea (Bryan, 2026-09-24) – NOT approved to build yet

## Bowl bonus level
- Reward, not always available: unlocks after an 80%+ round or every 3rd round.
- Short: 8–10 problems. Drop into a bowl; problem at the bottom; big trick on the way up.
- Each right answer = higher up the wall. A miss loses some height (doesn't end the run).

## Space launch
- Perfect bowl run launches the rider into outer space. Great snapshot-album moment.

## New planets (added worlds, NOT a reset)
- First perfect bowl unlocks Moon Park / Mars Skatepark: low gravity, floatier tricks.
- New space rides and gear (astronaut helmet, hover board, etc.).
- Harder math on planets (2-digit x 2-digit, division).
- Keep all coins, gear, album. Never "start over."

Build after: places + BMX + unlock ladder/Goals screen are live and feel right.
